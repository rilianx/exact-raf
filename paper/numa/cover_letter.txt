Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Exact multiplication of reduced affine forms", by Ignacio Araya,
Daniel Miranda, Victor Reyes, Juan Geraldo and Eva Ponce, for consideration in Numerical Algorithms.

Reduced affine forms keep a fixed number of noise symbols and accumulate all the approximation
errors in a single error term; the width of the enclosure of a product determines the quality of
range bounds and of the linear relaxations used in interval branch-and-bound solvers. In this
journal, Skalna and Hladik (Numer. Algorithms 76, 2017) computed in linear time the exact range of
the quadratic part of the product. Our manuscript shows that their multiplication is not exact in
general when both operands carry errors, and contributes:

- a multiplication that computes the exact range of the whole nonlinear part of the product in
  O(n log n) time, by a single walk along the boundary of a planar zonotope, with a proof that it
  gives the narrowest enclosure with the usual linear part;
- a proof that it coincides with the multiplication of Skalna and Hladik when one operand has no
  error or a zero center, and is at most 3/2 times narrower otherwise, a tight bound; a closed-form
  exact range of the square;
- a rigorous floating-point implementation of both multiplications in the affine arithmetic of
  the Ibex library;
- experiments on range enclosures and in the global optimizer IbexOpt, where both multiplications
  reduce the number of nodes about four times on problems dominated by products, and the new one
  improves further where the theory predicts a gain.

The code, data and scripts that produce all the tables and figures are publicly available at
https://github.com/rilianx/exact-raf.

The manuscript is original, has not been published before and is not under consideration for
publication elsewhere. All the authors have approved the submission. The authors have no competing
interests to declare.
